# Accuracy — local review edition

Open **Accuracy.html** in Edge, Chrome or another modern browser. On this Windows computer, you can also double-click **Open Accuracy.cmd**. Keep using the same browser and file location to retain your local progress.

This edition runs entirely on your computer. The website has not been deployed, and your supplied PDFs have not been uploaded or bundled into the app.

## What to review

1. Open **Mock tests**, choose a set, and read the start instructions.
2. Select, clear and flag answers. Switch sections or use the question navigator.
3. Try **Save & leave**, then resume. Reload the browser to check persistence.
4. Submit a test and inspect its score, accuracy, coverage, and all four option explanations.
5. Open **Learn & Tricks**, choose a topic, and expand a method. Try the worked example before revealing its reasoning, then use **Practise this topic**.
6. Use **Question bank** to search concepts. Bookmark questions and visit **My revision**.
7. Open **Study guide** to export a progress backup. Import restores that backup after confirmation.
8. Try a phone-size window and the sidebar’s animation toggle.

## Exam format

The mock structure follows the Telangana SI preliminary format: 200 questions, 180 minutes, 100 Arithmetic & Reasoning and 100 General Studies. Marking is +1 correct, −0.20 wrong and 0 unanswered. See the official [29 July 2026 notification](https://www.tgprb.in/SI_PC_2026/SI%20(Civil%20et%20al)%202026%20Notification%20dated%2029-07-2026.pdf), printed pages 19–20. Topic allocations within sections are editorial practice choices.

This is an English preliminary preparation edition. It does not simulate the separate mains language/descriptive papers. An 85% accuracy goal is a practice target, not a promise about future exam performance.

## Data and privacy

- Answers and bookmarks use browser local storage. Clearing browser data can remove progress.
- Export backups regularly; they contain your test history.
- The timer continues when you leave or close the app. Expired attempts submit saved answers when the app next runs.
- Use one browser tab for a timed mock.
- This local edition has no account sign-in or cloud sync. People sharing your browser profile can access its saved progress.
- Source links open external reference websites only when you click them. The app uses no remote fonts, analytics or external scripts.

## Content review

Questions are original practice material. Complete questions are assigned to one set only. Arithmetic and reasoning currently include 2,000 numerical/scenario variants drawn from 50 method families and 62 problem forms; these are not 2,000 different problem structures. Concepts and methods therefore recur. General Studies includes dated current affairs; it is not an exhaustive current-events feed. The bank has automated structural checks and selected factual review, but it is not independently certified by an exam subject-matter panel. Please review content quality, repetition of methods, and difficulty as well as the interface before approving a release.

The editable app is in the **accuracy** folder. **Accuracy.html** is the self-contained version. The earlier extracted-paper search and original PDF resources are intentionally excluded from this repository.
